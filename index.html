Why GWAZU

BUILT FOR CHAMPIONS

Born in Kampala's suburbs in 2017 — GWAZU (from gwas, slang for "homes") is a collective of friends turned into a full sportswear & apparel agency.

Unmatched Speed

14 business days for branded sportswear, 1–10 days for apparel. Nearly double the speed of competitors.

No Minimum Order

A single jersey or a full squad's kit — if you can dream it, we produce it. Every order gets the same care.

Free Citywide Delivery

Every full-price order within the Greater Kampala Metropolitan Area ships to your door — free.

Personalized Design

Every kit is created around your identity — your colors, your badge, your story. Aesthetics meets function.
